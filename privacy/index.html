Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy explains what information DarkSky Studios ("we", "us") collects when you play our games, Blindfold Chess, Study With Me: Online and 1vs1 (the "Games"), or visit this website, and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

Through Steam. When you play, Steam provides us with your public profile information, such as your Steam ID, display name and avatar. We use it to identify you in the Games and to show you to other players.

Gameplay data. For online features we process data needed to run them, such as the match or room you join, your in-game activity, and the settings you choose.

Technical data. We may collect your IP address, device and operating system type, game version and crash reports, to run the service and fix problems.

Communication. If you contact us, we keep your message and email address so we can reply.

2. How we use it

  • To run the Games and their online features
  • To keep the Games secure and prevent abuse or cheating
  • To fix bugs and improve the Games
  • To answer your questions and support requests

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Sharing

Other players can see your Steam display name and avatar and what you do in shared matches or rooms. We share data with service providers that host and operate the Games (such as hosting and analytics providers), only as needed to provide the service, or when required by law. Steam is operated by Valve Corporation and is governed by its own privacy policy.

4. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above, then delete or anonymize it. You can ask us to delete your data at any time (see section 6).

5. Children

The Games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you think a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU/EEA or Turkey), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To use any of these rights, email us at the address below.

About the game

Studying alone can feel lonely. Study With Me Online puts you in a shared online space with other people who are working on their own goals, so you can stay focused and keep each other company.

Pick a room, sit down, and start your session.

Features

  • Study alongside other players in shared online rooms
  • Focus sessions and break timers
  • Cozy, distraction-free atmosphere
  • Made by an independent studio
